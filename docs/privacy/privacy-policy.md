## 2. Summary

The app is a reading client for a **Nextcloud server that you enter yourself**. The developer runs no server for the app and receives **no data at all** from you or your device. The app contains **no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no crash reporting**.

## 3. Data the app processes

To work, the app processes on your device:

- **Credentials:** the address of your Nextcloud server, your user name and an app password. Your server creates the app password when you log in through the browser (Nextcloud Login Flow). It is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore. The app never sees your actual Nextcloud password.
- **Library data:** titles, authors, series, genres, tags, covers and other details of your books, and your shelves.
- **Book files** you download for offline reading, stored in the app's private storage.
- **Reading data:** reading position, reading status, ratings, highlights and notes, and a device name of your choice (default: the device model) that is sent with the reading position so you can see on other devices where you stopped.
- **App settings** such as theme and reader settings. These stay on the device.

## 4. Where data is transferred

Data is transferred **only between your device and the Nextcloud server you enter in the app**, always encrypted over HTTPS. The operator of that server is responsible for processing on it: you, if you run the server yourself, otherwise your cloud provider. Their privacy policy applies.

Data is never shared with the developer or with any other third party.

Links inside books open in your device's browser only after you confirm. The privacy policies of the websites you visit apply there.

## 5. Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** connection to your server; downloads on Wi-Fi only if you choose so.
- **Notifications:** progress and completion of downloads. You can turn them off in the Android settings at any time.
- **Foreground service (data sync):** large downloads continue reliably while the app is in the background.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or your other files.

## 6. Retention and deletion

All data stays on the device until you delete it:

- **"Remove account"** in the app deletes all local data of that account (library, downloaded books and credentials) and revokes the app password on your server.
- **Uninstalling** the app deletes all local data.
- Downloaded books can be deleted at any time under "Downloads".

You manage data on your Nextcloud server, such as reading position, highlights and shelves, on the server itself.

## 7. Legal basis

Processing on the device serves only to provide the features you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The developer does not process any personal data.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the developer receives no data from you, please contact the operator of your server about data stored there. For questions about the app, use the email address above.

## 9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

## 10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the legal situation changes. The version published here applies.
