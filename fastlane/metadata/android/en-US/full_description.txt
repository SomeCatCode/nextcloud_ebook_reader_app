Read your e-books and comics from a folder on your device or straight from your own Nextcloud, on the go and offline.

The app also works without any server: just pick a folder on your phone or SD card, and the app builds your library from it with titles, authors, series and covers. If you have a Nextcloud with the "E-Book Reader" app, connect it as well and keep your library, shelves, series and reading position in sync across your devices and the browser.

NO SERVER NEEDED: LOCAL FOLDERS
• One or more folders on the device as separate libraries, including sub-folders
• Titles, authors, series, description and cover are read from EPUB, FB2 and CBZ
• Reading position, highlights and notes stay on the device
• No storage permission: the app only sees the folders you choose

COPY AND MOVE
• Copy books to another account, e.g. from a folder on your phone to your Nextcloud or the other way round
• Or move them: copy, then delete the original
• Reading position and highlights come along

LIBRARY
• EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2 and comics as CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT
• Grid and list view, search, sorting, "Continue reading"
• Filters like on the web: genres and tags as a tree, authors, series, formats, missing metadata; include or exclude terms
• Hide finished books, reading status and ratings
• Create and manage shelves and smart shelves, series in volume order

READING
• Comfortable reader with light, sepia and dark themes, font size, line spacing and margins
• Paginated or scrolling, table of contents, tap zones, volume keys to turn pages
• Comics with zoom (pinch, double tap), two-page spreads and right-to-left reading for manga
• Highlights and notes, synced with the web app
• Reading position syncs automatically; if another device is further ahead, the app asks
• E-ink mode with high contrast and no animations

OFFLINE
• Make books, whole shelves or series available offline
• New volumes of pinned series and shelves are downloaded automatically
• Downloads on Wi-Fi only if you like

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS
• Several Nextcloud accounts and local folders, separate or as one combined library
• Secure login through the browser (Nextcloud Login Flow); the app never sees your password

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Local libraries stay on your device; with Nextcloud, your data travels only between your device and your own server.

NEXTCLOUD (OPTIONAL)
For sync, shelves and reading in the browser: a Nextcloud server with the "E-Book Reader" app version 0.7.0 or later (Nextcloud App Store or GitHub). Local folders need no server.
