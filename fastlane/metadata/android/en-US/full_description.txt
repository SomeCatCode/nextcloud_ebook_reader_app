Read your e-books and comics straight from your own Nextcloud, on the go and offline.

The app connects to your Nextcloud server running the "E-Book Reader" app. Your library, shelves, series and reading position stay in sync across your devices and the browser.

LIBRARY
• EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2 and comics as CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT
• Grid and list view, search, sorting, "Continue reading"
• Filters like on the web: genres and tags as a tree, authors, series, formats, missing metadata; include or exclude terms
• Hide finished books, reading status and ratings
• Create and manage shelves and smart shelves, series in volume order

READING
• Comfortable reader with light, sepia and dark themes, font size, line spacing and margins
• Paginated or scrolling, table of contents, tap zones, volume keys to turn pages
• Comics with zoom (pinch, double tap), two-page spreads and right-to-left reading for manga
• Highlights and notes, synced with the web app
• Reading position syncs automatically; if another device is further ahead, the app asks
• E-ink mode with high contrast and no animations

OFFLINE
• Make books, whole shelves or series available offline
• New volumes of pinned series and shelves are downloaded automatically
• Downloads on Wi-Fi only if you like

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS
• Several Nextcloud accounts, separate or as one combined library
• Secure login through the browser (Nextcloud Login Flow); the app never sees your password

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your data travels only between your device and your own Nextcloud server.

REQUIREMENT
A Nextcloud server with the "E-Book Reader" app version 0.7.0 or later (Nextcloud App Store or GitHub). The app does not work without this server app.
